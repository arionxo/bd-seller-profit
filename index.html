Calculation assumptions

Returned products are assumed recoverable. Ad cost is measured per placed order, including returned orders. The extra return loss covers costs not already included in successful-order expenses. Taxes, salaries, damaged inventory and fixed overhead are excluded unless you account for them in your inputs.
