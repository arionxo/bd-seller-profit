This policy describes how BD Seller Profit (bdsellerprofit.xyz) currently works.

Calculations stay in your browser

All calculations run in your browser using JavaScript on the page. The numbers you enter are not sent to any server operated by this site, and they are not saved. When you reload or close the page, the example values come back.

Cookies and local storage

The site's own code does not set cookies and does not use your browser's local storage or session storage. Your language choice is kept only in the page address (for example ?lang=bn). Google's advertising code, described below, may set or read cookies in your browser.

Google AdSense advertising code

The site's pages load Google AdSense code from Google's servers so that the site can show ads served by Google. When this code loads, Google receives information such as your IP address, browser details and the address of the page you are viewing. The site has no analytics, and apart from this Google code it loads no other third-party scripts, external fonts or libraries.

Copy and Share buttons

"Copy results" writes the results to your device's clipboard only when you press it. "Share tool" opens your device's share menu, or copies the page link if sharing is not supported. What you share, and with whom, is up to you.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. Like most web hosts, GitHub may collect technical information such as your IP address and browser details when you visit, for security and to run the service. This is handled under GitHub's Privacy Statement. This site's operator does not receive individual visitor data from it.

Email

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. We use it only to reply, and we do not sell it.

Advertising cookies and your choices

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Google and its partners may also use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to measure ads and to limit how often you see them.

You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings. Read more about how Google uses cookies in advertising and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Children

The site is a business tool and is not directed at children.
